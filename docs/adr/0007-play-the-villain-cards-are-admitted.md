# Play-the-villain cards are admitted

**Status:** accepted (2026-09-27), merged from the four fun-grill runs. Deliberately reopens the
gamification merge's *"play the villain card → out of scope"* note.

We decided **Villain Cards** are allowed as content: two to start — a Stage-4 phone-shop shift that
has the player sell the four-payment split to a friend, and a Stage-5 app referral that pays the
player for bringing friends in. The player is the one selling; the predatory terms are theirs to
offer; a **Thread** carries the buyer's consequence back. Gains are small, the effect vocabulary is
unchanged, and the game never flags the choice as wrong — the consequence teaches. This is the
research's strongest anti-moralising device: the mechanic is learned from the inside instead of being
explained. A third card is optional and playtest-gated.

## Considered options

- **Keep the exclusion** — loses the most direct answer to "less like a class".
- **Role-play or morality systems** — rejected: new systems and a wrong-choice flag; both banned.
- **Two bounded cards, consequence-carrying, playtest-gated (chosen).**

## Consequences

- The cards are content review, not mechanics: any future villain card must keep the fence (small
  gain, costlier Thread, no shaming, no maxim).
- The Better-Choices Proof is untouched: the gain cannot make a proof-worsening choice attractive
  beyond its small amount, and the Thread makes its cost felt.
- A playtest looks specifically for "the game told me off" — a failing card is cut without residue.
