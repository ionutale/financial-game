# The deck is a living surface, under a balance and variety harness

**Status:** accepted (2026-09-27), merged from the four fun-grill runs.

We decided the content freeze is lifted **for content only**: cards and Threads may be added,
repaired and rewritten — cast-led social-pressure cards, 4–6 new Threads, windfalls, extra risk
moments, and villain cards (ADR-0007) — under three rules: **no new mechanics** (the effect
vocabulary, draw, reducer and economy numbers are frozen), **no filler** (every card carries a
consequence), and **two defensible options** per decision card, with at least one True Dilemma in
three per Stage. Every content change must pass a **balance + variety harness**: ~100 seeded Runs per
scripted policy through the real reducer, asserting the Outcome-Band distribution, the final-net-worth
spread and the year-5 obligations-to-income ratio stay inside bands recorded from the current deck,
plus a variety threshold (mean drawn-card overlap falls). Cards are tuned; the economy is never
adjusted to fit a card.

## Considered options

- **Keep the deck frozen** — thin Stages keep feeling like a syllabus of the same few cards.
- **Grow freely** — drifts the balance and the Proof.
- **Growth and repair under a hard harness (chosen).**

## Consequences

- Content pull requests must run the harness; a red band is a rejected card, not a retuned economy.
- The True-Dilemma rule is an authoring gate, checked in review — no automated test can judge a
  dilemma.
- The lean year stays lean: no card may guarantee year-5 survival; shocks stay bounded.
